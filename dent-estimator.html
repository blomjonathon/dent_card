Dent Estimator

1 credit card = 1 labor hour
  1. Tap Open Camera.
  2. Hold a credit card flat on the panel next to the dent.
  3. Move the phone until the card exactly fills the yellow outline.
  4. Take the card away, keep the phone still, and tap Capture.

The card is only used to set the distance. It doesn't need to be in the photo.

Uploaded photos must show a credit card lying flat on the panel near the dent. You'll tap the two ends of its long edge to set the scale.

Smaller outline = phone farther back (for bigger dents). Keep the phone square to the panel.
